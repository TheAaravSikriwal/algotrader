# Source 01 — "Top 1-to-7 Day Strategies" (text list, origin unstated)

Claims and rules exactly as given:

- **High-Yield Savings Accounts (HYSAs) & Money Market Funds.** "Earning daily
  interest with zero risk." Cash earns "a tiny fraction every single day", can
  be withdrawn anytime.
- **Treasury Bills / Cash ETFs.** Buying short-term government-backed assets or
  ultra-short bond ETFs "like SGOV or BIL" that pay daily accrued interest.
  "Very safe and liquid."
- **Swing Trading Liquid Stocks.** Buy stocks with high trading volume showing
  "strong technical momentum", aiming to sell for a small profit "within 2 to 5
  days."
- **Crypto Staking / Flexible Earn Programs.** Depositing stablecoins into
  flexible crypto yield programs to earn daily rewards, "though this carries
  platform and market risk."

Stated risks:

- Short-term gains taxed at higher ordinary income rates; trading fees can wipe
  out small profits.
- "Trying to time stocks or crypto for a 1-to-7-day gain is risky. Prices can
  drop quickly before you can sell."
- "Safe choices like HYSAs will only yield a tiny fraction of a percent over a
  7-day window."

No monetisation attached. No performance claims. No specific entry/exit rules
given for the swing-trading item.
